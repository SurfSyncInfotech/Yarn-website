Our Heritage

Three Generations. One Thread.

Founded in Surat in 1987, Rajvastra began as a small yarn trading house.

Today we run spinning, dyeing and finishing units and work with 14 handloom clusters. Our promise is simple: exceptional material, honest provenance, and fair rewards for the hands that make it.

Our Journey

Four Decades, One Thread

1987

A yarn trading house is born

Rajvastra opens in Surat, supplying cotton yarn to local mills.

1999

Dye house & silk reeling

We add in-house dyeing and begin direct partnerships with silk reeling units.

2008

Handloom clusters join

Long-term programmes begin with weavers in Varanasi, Phulia and Bagru.

2016

Export desk opens

Dedicated team for private label, documentation and global logistics.

2024

Natural dye & organic range

GOTS-certified cotton and natural indigo lines launched.

2026

2,000+ clients in 30 countries

Designers, boutiques and mills return season after season.

A Note From Our Founder

Cloth Is Memory. We Simply Help It Travel.

“When my grandfather started trading yarn in Surat in 1987, he used to say that every thread carries the hands that made it. Four decades on, that is still how we work: we know the farms, the reelers, the dyers and the weaver families behind every metre we sell.

Our job is to protect that chain of craft and make it easy for designers anywhere in the world to use it. If we do that well, the cloth will speak for itself.”

Our Craft Process

Six Stages of Quality, In Depth

01

Fibre sourcing

Cotton, silk, wool and zari are selected at origin and graded for length, purity and strength.

02

Spinning & reeling

Fibre is spun or reeled to exact counts and twists on modern machines and traditional charkhas.

03

Dyeing

Natural and azo-free dye lots are developed, lab-dipped and colour-matched in our Surat dye house.

04

Weaving

Handloom and power-loom partners weave to our specifications, with design review at every stage.

05

Finishing

Soft-washing, calendering and hand finishing give each fabric its signature handle.

06

Inspection & packing

A three-gate quality check precedes export-grade packing and dispatch.

Fibre to Fabric

How Every Material Is Made

Each material follows a documented route: fibre selection at source, spinning or reeling, dyeing in our Surat dye house, weaving by partner clusters, hand finishing and a three-gate quality inspection.

You can request the full production passport for any lot, showing where it was grown, who spun or wove it and which tests it passed.

  • Fibre graded for staple length, denier and purity
  • Spun, twisted and dyed under controlled conditions
  • Woven on handlooms and modern looms by trained artisans
  • Finished, inspected and packed to export standards

Custom Development

Your Brief, Our Looms

Need an exclusive count, a bespoke motif, a custom jacquard or a proprietary shade? Our design desk develops yarns and fabrics to your brief, from first sketch through lab dip, sample metre and pilot lot to bulk production.

Minimum quantities for custom development start low so that boutiques and new labels can create something truly their own.

  • Custom counts, twists and blends
  • Exclusive motifs, borders and jacquards
  • Pantone-matched natural and azo-free dyeing
  • Private labelling and bespoke packing
